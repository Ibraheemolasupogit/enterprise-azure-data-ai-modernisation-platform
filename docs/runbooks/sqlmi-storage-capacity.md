# SQL MI Storage and Capacity Alert

## Trigger

Storage threshold or growth trend alert.

## Triage

- Check storage used, growth rate, largest tables, and recent load activity.
- Review job history and migration activity.
- Confirm backup/retention or audit growth changes.

## Evidence

- Metric trend.
- Largest object summary.
- Recent deployment or data load.

## Action

- Increase storage only after approval.
- Review retention and archival candidates.
- Route index/storage optimization to later performance milestone.

## Escalation

Escalate if projected exhaustion falls inside the next business day.

## Validation

- Capacity headroom restored.
- Growth alert clears.

## Closure

Record capacity decision and follow-up review date.
